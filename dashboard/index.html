Resource Distribution Explanation

The Risk Scores are calculated and normalized based on the monthly model predictions, with values ranging from 0 to 10.

60% of hours (480) are equally shared for all LSOAs within the ward for basic coverage.

40% of hours (320) are used as reinforcement for higher risk areas.

Base hours = 480 / number of LSOAs within ward

Reinforcement hours = (Risk score of LSOA / Total Risk of LSOAs within ward) × 320

Final Allocation = Base hours + Reinforcement hours

The Final Allocation is rounded to 2 for easier allocation of 2 hour patrolling shifts.
